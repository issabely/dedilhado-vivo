How to tune the guitar

  1. Choose Guitar in the app and open the tab Tuner.
  2. Play an open string and let it ring. The tuner finds the string on its own.
  3. Needle to the left: tighten the string by turning the tuning peg slowly.
  4. Needle to the right: loosen it a little below and bring it back up to the center.
  5. Repeat from the 6th string (the thickest) to the 1st. In the "Tune the 6 strings" lesson, the app guides you string by string.

How to tune the violin

First tune the A, then D, G and E. For small adjustments use the fine tuner on the tailpiece; the peg only when the string is way off.

Frequently asked questions

Is the online tuner free?

Yes, completely free and no sign-up. It works in your phone or computer browser.

Is the microphone sound recorded?

No. The sound is analyzed on your own device, in real time, and is never recorded or sent to any server.

Why doesn't the tuner work on my phone?

Check that you've allowed the microphone for the site. On iPhone, open it in Safari; inside some apps (like Instagram's browser) the microphone may be blocked.

What is standard guitar tuning?

From the thickest string to the thinnest: E A D G B E.
