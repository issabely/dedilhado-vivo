How to start on piano

  1. Find C. It's the white key just to the left of each group of two black keys. Middle C is near the center.
  2. Number your fingers. Thumb = 1, index = 2, middle = 3, ring = 4, pinky = 5, on both hands.
  3. C position. Right hand with the thumb on middle C and one finger on each key up to G.
  4. First songs with five notes: Twinkle, Twinkle, Little Star, Ode to Joy.
  5. Thumb under. To play the whole scale, your thumb passes under the other fingers.

Frequently asked questions

Can you learn piano on your phone?

You can learn the notes, the fingers and reading. To practice, use a real keyboard: the app listens through the microphone or gets the notes from a keyboard plugged in via USB.

What's the first song to play on the piano?

Twinkle, Twinkle, Little Star in C position: it uses only five keys and one finger for each.

How do you know which finger to use on piano?

Fingers are numbered from 1 (thumb) to 5 (pinky). The app shows the finger number above each note on the staff.
