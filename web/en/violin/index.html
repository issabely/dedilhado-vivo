Where to start on the violin

  1. Get the bow ready. Tighten the hair to about 5 mm from the stick and apply rosin.
  2. Tune the strings. G, D, A and E. Tune the A first, with the app's tuner.
  3. Open strings with long bows. Keep the bow straight, parallel to the bridge, without pressing: the sound comes from the weight of your arm.
  4. Fingers 1, 2 and 3 on the A string. B, C♯ and D. Together with the D string, you get the D major scale.
  5. First songs. Twinkle, Twinkle, Little Star and Ode to Joy in D, changing bow direction on every note.

Frequently asked questions

Can you learn violin on your own?

You can start on your own with a good guide and something to check your tuning. The app's microphone does that and tells you if a note is sharp or flat. Lessons with a teacher help a lot with posture.

What is the first scale to learn on the violin?

D major, on the D and A strings, with fingers 1, 2 and 3. It's the most natural scale for the hand in 1st position.

What do ⊓ and V mean in violin sheet music?

⊓ is a down-bow (pull, from frog to tip) and V is an up-bow (push). The app shows the direction for each note.

Does the app have a violin tuner?

Yes, free, with the G, D, A and E strings and tips on when to use the fine tuner or the tuning peg.
