How to get your first sound on the flute

  1. Just the flute headjoint. Start without the body: it's lighter and you focus only on your air.
  2. Rest it on your chin. The edge of the lip plate sits in the crease between your lower lip and chin. Your lower lip covers about 1/4 of the hole.
  3. Lips shaped like saying "pu". Relaxed lips, as if softly saying "pu", with a small oval opening. No smiling and no puffed cheeks.
  4. Aim at the edge. The air goes across the hole and hits the edge on the other side. If you only get air, roll the flute in or out just a little.
  5. Start with your tongue. Each note starts with a soft "tu".
  6. Practice with a bottle. Blowing across an empty bottle until it whistles is exactly the same motion.

Frequently asked questions

What's the first note to learn on the flute?

B. It uses only the left thumb and index finger plus the right pinky on the E♭ key, and it comes out with relaxed air.

Why does only air come out of my flute?

The air isn't hitting the edge of the lip plate. Cover a bit more of the hole with your lower lip, make the opening between your lips smaller and slowly roll the flute until the sound appears.

How long does it take to get a sound out of the flute?

It ranges from a few minutes to a few days. Practicing with just the headjoint and with a bottle speeds it up a lot.

Does the app show flute fingerings?

Yes. All of them, from C4 to C7, on the Boehm system, with a drawing of the flute, the hands and tips for each note.

Does it work with a recorder?

Dedilhado Vivo is made for the flute. The recorder has different fingerings.
