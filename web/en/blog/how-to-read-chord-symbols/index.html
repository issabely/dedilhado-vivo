How to read chord symbols (complete guide)

A chord symbol like F#m7(♭5)/C can look scary, but it's just a short phrase read from left to right. Here's the complete dictionary.

The reading order

Every chord symbol follows the same order:

Note · accidental · type · seventh · extensions · /bass

Example: F♯m7(♭5)/C = F (F) + ♯ (sharp) + m (minor) + 7 (minor seventh) + (♭5) (diminished fifth) + /C (C in the bass).

5. The slash: the bass

What comes after the slash is the lowest note. C/E = C chord with E in the bass. D/F# = D with F♯ in the bass. Am/G = A minor with G in the bass.

Sometimes the bass is a note from the chord itself (inversion); sometimes it's a note from outside, creating a walking bass line: C – C/B – Am – Am/G – F.

Chords as scale degrees (I, IV, V)

Musicians also write chords by degree (Roman numerals), which works in any key. In C major, I – V – VIm – IV = C – G – Am – F. In G, the same progression is G – D – Em – C. This is explained in diatonic chords.

Quick summary
  • Order: note, accidental, type, seventh, extensions, /bass.
  • Letter alone = major; m = minor.
  • 7 = minor seventh; 7M = major seventh.
  • ° = diminished; ø or m7(♭5) = half-diminished.
  • Numbers in parentheses = added or altered notes.
  • / = bass note (C/E: E in the bass).

Frequently asked questions

What does the m mean in a chord symbol?

Minor. Am = A minor, Em = E minor.

What does 7M mean?

Major seventh: the major chord plus the note a half step below the octave. C7M = C E G B.

What does the slash mean in a chord symbol, like in C/E?

The note after the slash goes in the bass. C/E is a C chord with E as the lowest note.

What does the ° symbol mean in a chord symbol?

Diminished. In popular music it usually means the diminished seventh: C° = C E♭ G♭ A.
